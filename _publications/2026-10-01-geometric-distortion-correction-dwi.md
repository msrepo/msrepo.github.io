We study whether large-scale pretraining (a self-supervised model and a generative model) improves deep learning-based correction of geometric distortion in single-phase-encoding diffusion-weighted MRI, compared with a non-pretrained baseline. Pretrained models do better, but applying the best one to data from a low-resource setting shows transferability problems, which registration to a common standard space helps to reduce.
