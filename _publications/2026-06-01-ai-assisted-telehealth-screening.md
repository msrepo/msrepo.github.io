Vision- and hearing-threatening diseases cause preventable disability, especially where specialists and screening facilities are scarce. AI-assisted screening and telehealth could widen early detection, but deploying them in paper-based workflows is hard and little field experience has been documented. This paper reports field observations from developing and deploying such a system, the challenges met, and the way forward.
